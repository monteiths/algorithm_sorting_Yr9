Sorting Algorithms

Three ways to sort the same five cards, one step at a time.

01Bubble SortCompare neighbours. Swap them if they are out of order.02Selection SortFind the smallest card that is still unsorted and put it in the next position.03QuicksortChoose a pivot, then group the cards into less than, equal to and greater than the pivot.
