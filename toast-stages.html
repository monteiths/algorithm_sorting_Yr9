Instructions

Drag a card, or select it and then select a space. Scroll to see all 21.

Your order

Use the stage headings. Put one instruction in each numbered space.

Why one exact sequence?

A completed algorithm can look obvious. Creating its precise steps from scratch is harder.

Another algorithm might also make toast. Here, your challenge is to reconstruct this particular algorithm exactly. A deterministic algorithm follows the same steps and gives the same result each time when its inputs and conditions are the same.

To move an unchecked card, drag it to another space or back to Instructions. Correct steps lock after checking. Start again clears all progress. Keyboard: select a card with Enter or Space, then select a numbered space.
