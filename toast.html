TECHQUITYYEAR 9 · PROBLEMS & INSTRUCTIONS

SEQUENCING CHALLENGE

🍞Make the toast.

Rebuild the exact toast-making sequence from Techquity, from first to last. Every step must be in its specified position. If even one is wrong, you will see “Incorrect” and all the cards will be shuffled back.

Instructions

Drag a card, or select it and then select a space.

Your order

Start at 1. Put one instruction in each space.

Why one exact sequence?

A completed algorithm can look obvious. Creating its precise steps from scratch is harder.

Another algorithm might also make toast. Here, your challenge is to reconstruct this particular algorithm exactly. A deterministic algorithm follows the same steps and gives the same result each time when its inputs and conditions are the same.

To move a placed card, drag it to another space or back to Instructions. Keyboard: select a card with Enter or Space, then select a numbered space.
